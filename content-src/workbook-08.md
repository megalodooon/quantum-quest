# Part 8 workbook: classical mechanics

Use g = 9.8 m/s² unless told otherwise. Highlight: in 8.34 you find the true path of a thrown ball by minimizing the action yourself.

### 8.1 Distance and displacement

**Checks**

- [ ] I can tell distance from displacement.

**Exercises**

1. You run one lap of a 400 m track. Find your distance and displacement.
2. You walk 5 km north then 12 km east. Find your displacement.

### 8.2 Speed, velocity and acceleration

**Checks**

- [ ] I can explain why turning at constant speed is acceleration.

**Exercises**

2. A car goes from 0 to 100 km/h in 4 s. Find its average acceleration in m/s² and in units of g.
3. You drive to a town at 60 km/h and back at 40 km/h. What is your average speed? (It is not 50.)
