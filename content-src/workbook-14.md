# Part 14 workbook: the experiments that broke classical physics

Use hc ≈ 1240 eV·nm. Highlight: in 14.3b Moseley's simple rule predicts copper's X-ray energy to within 1%.

### 14.1 Discovering the electron

**Checks**

- [ ] I can explain how Thomson measured e/m and what it showed.

**Exercises**

1. Thomson found e/m about 1800 times larger than for hydrogen ions. What did that imply about the electron's mass?
2. The electron's e/m is 1.76 × 10¹¹ C/kg and the proton's 9.58 × 10⁷ C/kg. Find their mass ratio.
3. Which everyday device used cathode rays for decades?
