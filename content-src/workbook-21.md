# Part 21 workbook: particle and nuclear physics

Constants you'll need: 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c², m_p = 938.3 MeV, m_n = 939.6 MeV, ħc = 197.3 MeV·fm, and B[T] = p[GeV]/(0.2998 × r[m]). These exercises use real experiments: you'll count the Sun's neutrinos passing through your thumb, steer LHC protons, and work out how a single cosmic ray compares with a thrown baseball.
