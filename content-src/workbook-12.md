Use c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s and γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²). Highlight: in 12.15 the rapidity for 0.6c turns out to be exactly ln 2.

### 12.1 The Michelson-Morley experiment

**Checks**

- [ ] I can explain what the experiment expected and what it found.

**Exercises**

### 12.4 Time dilation

**Checks**
