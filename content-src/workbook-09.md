# Part 9 workbook: waves, oscillations and optics

Use hc ≈ 1240 eV·nm and the speed of sound 343 m/s. Highlight: in 9.26 you find that a matter wave's group velocity is exactly the particle's classical velocity.

**Checks**

- [ ] I can tell transverse from longitudinal waves and say what a wave carries.

**Exercises**

2. Classify as transverse, longitudinal or both: sound in air, light, a guitar string, ocean surface waves, earthquake P and S waves.
